# Privacy Policy for Yummy 2

**Effective date: 28 August 2026**

Yummy 2 is a recipe-management application. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you. By using Yummy 2, you agree to this policy.

## Information we collect

The information collected depends on how you use the app:

- **Account information.** If you create an account or sign in, we collect your email address, a Firebase account identifier, and - when you sign in with Google - your Google display name and email address. We do not receive or store your Google password.
- **Profile information.** You may choose to provide a display name and phone number in the **My Info** area.
- **Recipe content.** We store the recipes you create, including their title, category, description, ingredients, directions, timings, temperatures, servings, notes, and any video links and descriptions you add.
- **Notification information.** If you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging processes a device notification token so that the app can deliver notifications. You can turn notifications off in your device settings.
- **Technical and usage information.** Firebase services may process limited technical information such as device and app identifiers, IP address, operating system, and app interaction information to operate, secure, and improve their services. The app does not use advertising identifiers for advertising and does not show personalised ads.

Yummy 2 does not request access to your device contacts, camera, location, photos, microphone, or call logs.

## How we use information

We use the information above to:

- create and secure your account and let you sign in;
- save, display, edit, search, and synchronise your recipes;
- show recipes that you choose to make public;
- associate public recipes with your display name;
- send app notifications when you have allowed them;
- maintain the reliability, security, and functionality of the app; and
- respond to support or deletion requests.

## Public recipes

Recipes are private by default. If you deliberately set a recipe to **Public**, its recipe content - including any links or descriptions you included - can be viewed by other Yummy 2 users. Your display name is shown with public recipes. Do not include personal, confidential, or sensitive information in a public recipe.

Your email address and phone number are not displayed by Yummy 2 as part of a public recipe.

## Sharing information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to run the app:

- **Google Firebase** provides authentication, Cloud Firestore database hosting, Cloud Messaging, and related technical services. Firebase processes data on our behalf to provide those services. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Firebase's data privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
- **Google Sign-In** is used when you choose to sign in with your Google account.
- When you use the app's share feature or open a video link, the information you choose to share or the link you open is handled by the receiving app or website under that party's privacy policy.

We may also disclose information if required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of Yummy 2, its users, or others.

## Data retention and deletion

We retain account, profile, and recipe data while your account is active and for as long as needed to provide the service. You can delete an individual recipe in the app at any time.

To request deletion of your account and associated personal data, email **bssam2012@gmail.com** with the subject line **"Yummy 2 account deletion request"** and include the email address used for your account. We may ask you to verify ownership of the account before deleting it. Once verified, we aim to complete the request within 30 days, except where retention is required by law or necessary for security, fraud prevention, or resolving disputes.

Deleting a recipe or account does not remove copies that another person has already shared, saved outside Yummy 2, or received through the operating system's sharing features.

## Security

We use Firebase and reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children

Yummy 2 is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information, contact us at the address below so that we can take appropriate action.

## Your choices

You can choose whether to create an account, what profile information to provide, whether a recipe is public, and whether to allow notifications. You can update recipe and profile information in the app, delete individual recipes, and request account deletion as described above.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or applicable requirements change. We will post the updated version at this page and change the effective date above. Continued use of Yummy 2 after an update means that you accept the updated policy.
